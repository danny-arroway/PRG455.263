Because Labour Day and Thanksgiving both fall on the Monday lab day, they collide directly with graded/required lab sessions:

1. **Week 1 Lab** (create GitHub repos, connect Claude Pro to VS Code, first AI-assisted commit) falls on **Labour Day, Sept 7**.
2. **Lab Test 1** (worth 22% of the final grade) falls on **Thanksgiving, Oct 12**.

Suggested handling (change as you see fit):
- **Week 1:** Since this lab is account/tool setup rather than hardware-dependent, assign it as homework due before the Sept 14 lab, with a short walkthrough during the Sept 10 lecture.
- **Lab Test 1:** Hold the test during the **Thursday, Oct 15 lecture slot** instead of that week's review lecture. Move the cumulative review content to the end of the Oct 8 lecture, or fold a short recap into the Oct 19 lab before Lab 6 begins.
